# Privacy Policy — Flip

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Flip (`dev.flip.clock`) is a flip clock for Android. It collects nothing about you.

## What Flip does not do

- **No data collection.** Flip does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.
- **No internet access.** The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission, so it cannot
  send anything off your device.
- **No ads, analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs.**
- **No permissions.** Flip requests no runtime or special permissions.

## Information used on your device only

To show what you ask it to, Flip reads a few things from Android itself. They are used only
on screen and never leave your device:

- **Time, date, time zone and 12/24-hour preference**, to display the clock.
- **Battery level and charging state**, when you turn on the battery indicator or set the
  screen to stay on while charging.
- **The time of your next alarm**, when you turn on "Next alarm". Flip learns only its time,
  not its label or which app set it.

## Your settings

Your clock and widget preferences, and the state of your timer and stopwatch, are stored
privately on your device. If you have Android backup turned on, Google may include your
clock settings in your device backup, as it does for other apps. Widget settings and
timer / stopwatch state are excluded because they belong to a specific device. You can
clear everything at any time with **Settings → Apps → Flip → Storage → Clear storage**.

## Links

The Privacy policy and Rate links in the app open your browser or the Play Store. Those
services have their own privacy policies.

## Children

Flip is suitable for everyone and collects no data from anyone, including children.
