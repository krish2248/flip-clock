## Full description (max 4000)

Flip turns your phone or tablet into a classic split-flap clock, the kind you remember from
old train stations and bedside radios. Every digit flips with real depth, shading and a
gentle bounce.

**Made to look right**
• Smooth, realistic flip animation with adjustable speed and bounce
• 11 themes: Auto, Material You, Midnight, Paper, AMOLED, Retro, Ocean, Forest, Rose, Sand, Neon
• 6 fonts, 3 card shapes and an adjustable clock size
• Choose a separator: dots, blinking, bars or none
• Optional mechanical "clack" sound

**Useful at a glance**
• 12- or 24-hour time, with or without seconds and leading zero
• Date in 7 styles, formatted for your language
• Your next alarm and battery level
• Settings preview live on the clock as you change them

**Timer and stopwatch, in flip style**
• Swipe between Clock, Timer and Stopwatch, or use the floating switcher
• Set a timer with quick presets or the arrows above each digit
• Shows when the timer will end, with a soft chime when time is up
• Stopwatch with hundredths and lap times (best lap highlighted)
• Long-press the app icon to jump straight to Timer or Stopwatch

**Bedside and desk friendly**
• Keep the screen on always, only while charging, or never
• Dims itself when idle; tap to wake
• Night mode with a soft red tint
• Burn-in protection gently shifts the clock
• Hide the status and navigation bars
• Use Flip as your screen saver while charging or docked

**Home-screen widgets**
• Flip Clock, Flip Clock & Date, Flip Calendar and Flip Mini
• 13 widget themes, including Glass and Material You
• Adjustable background opacity
• Always accurate, with no battery drain

**Private by design**
No ads. No tracking. No accounts. No internet permission. Flip requests no permissions.
